# Brag Plan: LLM Comparison Lab

## What is this app?
A local, free-price-verified comparison lab that gives two selected provider routes the same questions, retains durable request progress, and presents paired answers and narrow automatic checks without inventing an overall winner.

## The angle
Show a reproducible *decision*, not a leaderboard: choose the same questions, choose exact provider routes, check the request budget, then read paired evidence. The only results footage is an explicitly labeled synthetic fixture example. Starting a real run is NOT shown or performed.

## Hook (0–2s)
“Two models. The same questions.” Calm app-white title card with the actual guided comparison UI behind it.

## Key moments
- The live app's real question selection says its authored samples are for demonstration, not an established benchmark; 10 of 30 are selected.
- The live app visibly selects an OpenRouter Gemma route and an OpenCode Zen `big-pickle` route. Provider routing can affect results.
- The live review screen shows **10 selected questions × 2 routes = 20 initial requests**, retries 0 and an absolute cap; no provider generation is triggered by this video.
- The final results screenshot contains only synthetic fixture answers. Label it throughout its scene: “Illustrative fixture results — not measured model performance.” Highlight completion and paired answers; missing/failed are tracked separately. No overall winner.

## Outro
“Compare the evidence — not a made-up winner.” Add “Local app · live requests require your click.”

## User flow worth showing
Choose questions → choose Model A and Model B across providers → review request count before starting → inspect progress and side-by-side answers. Last scene is illustrative, not proof of a successful live run.

## Tone
- Preset: polished.
- Creative direction: calm annotated product walkthrough, not hype or speculative performance claims.
- Interpretation: slow framing of readable real UI, four restrained transitions, bold short labels and one warm amber accent.

## Format / Duration
Landscape 1920×1080 · **24 seconds** (2 + 5 + 5 + 5 + 7).

## Visual identity
- Background: `#f8fafc` / white `#ffffff` from the product.
- Accent: `#0f172a` navy and `#fef3c7` warm yellow/amber for cautions.
- Text: `#0f172a`.
- Fonts: system sans, Inter if locally available; otherwise Arial/system UI.
- Strongest visual: three-step comparison indicator, Model A/B cards, plain-language budget, paired answers.
- Capture provenance: `questions-live.png`, `live-model-selection.png`, `live-review.png` are live UI screenshots without generation; `results-fixture.png` is from a separate synthetic fixture demo. Never display keys or private account pages.

## Audio direction
- Warm unobtrusive original synthesized instrumental bed, fade in/out, quiet enough that text—not music—carries the explanation. No narration requested.
- No interaction sound: the explanatory screen text, rather than a synthetic click, carries the information. No celebratory “winner” sting.
- No third-party cue metadata or claimed beat sync. Motion follows reading time. Audio reactive optional/omitted for clarity; no distracting pulse.

## Storyboard

### Scene 1 — Same questions — 2s (0–2)
Actual guided comparison screen subdued behind large headline “Two models. The same questions.” Hook lands immediately; gentle push-in. Music fades up.

### Scene 2 — Questions — 5s (2–7)
Frame the actual step 1 screenshot and highlight “Try sample questions” plus “10 of 30”. On-screen line “1 · Choose questions” and short subline “Built-in samples are for practice, not a benchmark.” Hold both long enough to read. No cursor click needed.

### Scene 3 — Exact routes — 5s (7–12)
Show actual live Model A / Model B selections in screenshot, highlight each in sequence but retain both labels. “2 · Choose two provider routes.” Small line “Both get the same questions and shared instruction.” No fake performance claim.

### Scene 4 — Cost and progress — 5s (12–17)
Show actual live review budget. Call out “10 questions × 2 routes = 20 initial requests.” Second line “Retries 0 · cap shown before start.” Footer “After starting: track successful, failed, pending and active requests.” **Do not click Start.** Treat screenshots as preview, not generation evidence.

### Scene 5 — Paired answers — 7s (17–24)
Show saved synthetic result screenshot. Keep a clearly visible amber “ILLUSTRATIVE FIXTURES · NOT REAL RESULTS” badge across the entire scene; show completion counts and two answer cards on the same question. Copy “3 · Compare paired answers and coverage.” End line “No overall winner from a small sample.” Music fades out. Fixture answers do not demonstrate real model quality.

## Share copy (draft)
“LLM Comparison Lab: pick the same questions for two provider routes, see the request budget before starting, and inspect paired answers with failures kept visible. Video uses illustrative fixture results; no live generation was run for it.”
